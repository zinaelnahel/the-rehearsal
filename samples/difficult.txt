Fictional practice notes — Saying no to a family member
A family member asks you to help with errands this weekend. You have decided you cannot help this weekend.
You want them to ask before making plans that involve your time, rather than assuming you are available.
Practise saying no clearly, stating that boundary and holding it if they ask again. You do not have to give a detailed explanation or offer an alternative.
The family member may be disappointed. Their exact relationship to you, previous behaviour and agreement are not specified. Do not invent a family history or promise an outcome.
