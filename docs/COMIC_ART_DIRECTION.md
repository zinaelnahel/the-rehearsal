# The rehearsal storyboard

The comic supports the app's central idea: trying a conversation before having
it in real life. The three scenes correspond to the flat viewing, interview and
boundary-setting samples. The recurring character, storyboard frames, clapperboard,
editable “Take 02” speech bubbles and crossed-out hesitation make the practice
theme explicit. The artwork does not predict how another person will respond.

The illustration was created with the built-in image-generation tool. The web
asset is `public/images/rehearsal-comic.webp` (1536 × 1024, about 420 KiB).
Text, speech bubbles, scene labels and production annotations are HTML/CSS so
they translate into English, German and Spanish and remain accessible text.
The original generated PNG is preserved outside the repository; the WebP is
only a web export, with no changes to the illustration's composition.

## Final generation prompt

Create one original editorial comic illustration asset for a language conversation rehearsal web app, not a UI mockup. One single landscape image, overall aspect ratio 3:2, divided compositionally into EXACTLY THREE EQUAL-WIDTH VERTICAL PANELS from left to right, no drawn borders or gutters (the website will add borders). Each panel can be cropped independently at exactly one-third of image width. A warm, witty independent comic-zine look: confident imperfect dark navy ink contours, flat apricot, lilac, butter yellow and soft mint spot colors, subtle risograph halftone shading, warm ivory paper. Charming expressive ADULT characters with believable proportions and clean simple hands. Friendly and thoughtful, never childish, sarcastic or mocking. Same adult woman with a dark curly bob and lavender shirt appears in all three scenes. LEFT THIRD: a flat viewing, she holds a little notebook and talks to a friendly letting agent standing by an open apartment door; a small plant and house key establish the setting. CENTRE THIRD: a job interview, she sits across a little desk from a friendly interviewer, a small laptop and coffee cup; she is speaking, relaxed rather than triumphant. RIGHT THIRD: a personal boundary-setting conversation, she gently holds one open palm while speaking to an adult friend carrying one small moving box; both are considerate and calm. Compose each scene mainly in the LOWER THREE QUARTERS, keep the TOP QUARTER of each panel mostly blank warm ivory, with no faces there, for HTML speech bubbles overlaid by the website. Characters large enough to read when each panel is only 170px wide. Sparse charming scene details, a few ink conversation marks but NO speech bubbles drawn into the image. Absolutely NO text, letters, numerals, signs, logos or watermarks. No photorealism, gradients, generic corporate vector clipart or copyrighted characters. Deliver a finished beautiful standalone illustration, full bleed.
